How This Works

Snapshot timing. Official signals are frozen 45 to 120 minutes before scheduled tipoff, with a target capture approximately 75 minutes before the game.

Spread and totals signals. The models evaluate available sportsbook lines and publish only qualifying research signals. The displayed line, odds and model statistics reflect the frozen pregame snapshot.

Forward testing. Results shown on this dashboard come from forward-tracked signals. Settled signals move from the current slate into Pick History and are incorporated into the displayed performance record.

Research use. This dashboard is an experimental sports-modeling research project. Signals and performance statistics are presented for research and evaluation purposes.

This dashboard presents model research signals generated from a forward-testing process. Signals are not guarantees of future performance and should not be interpreted as financial or wagering advice.
